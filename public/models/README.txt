Drop real 3D garments here and the homepage hero switches to an interactive 3D viewer for that product:

  pants.glb   the track pants
  tee.glb     the T-shirt
  look.glb    the full look (shirt and pants together)

Without look.glb, the "Full look" tab stacks tee.glb over pants.glb in one scene when both exist.

Export as binary glTF (.glb), Draco or Meshopt compressed, ideally under 5 MB, with the garment upright and
roughly centred (it is auto-fitted and centred). Without a file the hero keeps the photo viewer.
